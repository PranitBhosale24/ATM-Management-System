# ATM-Management-System
A console-based ATM Management System developed using Python.

## Features
-Create Account
-Secure Login
-Deposit Money
-Withdraw Money
-Balance Enquiry
-Change Pin
-File Handling
-Exception Handling

## Technologies Used
-Python
-Object Oriented Programming
-File Handling

## Files
-ATM_Management_System.py
-Accounts.txt

## How to run
1. Open the project in Pycharm.
2. Run 'ATM_Management_System.py'.
3. Follow the on-screen menu.

## Flow of Program
1. First it will ask for 3 options . Type 1 to create account , Type 2 to login into account and Type 3 to exit the ATM
2. If you choose 1 then you have to create your account . Fill the required details which includes your name, your age(>18) ,your pin (4 Digit Number), your initial balance and after that you will get your account number.
3. After creating your account type 2 to login into your account . Enter your account number and password and access all the features of the ATM.
4. If already account is present in your file then you can skip  step 2.
5. If account is not present then it will tell to create an account.
6. After your login you will have 5 Options . Type 1 to deposit in your account , Type 2 to withdraw in your account , Type 3 to do enquiry of your balance , Type 4 to change your pin and Type 5 to exit the process
7. If you choose  4 which is to change your pin , You must write your old pin else it won't accept to change your pin.
8. To end the program after inner menu driven is ended . Type 3 to end the program.
